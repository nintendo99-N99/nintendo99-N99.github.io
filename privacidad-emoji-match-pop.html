Privacy Policy — Emoji Match Pop

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Emoji Match Pop ("the app") is a free, offline emoji-matching game developed by nintendo99, available on Google Play (com.nintendo99.emojimatchpop). This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: none.

Information we collect: none

  • The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.
  • The app does not require an account and has no login.
  • The app works 100% offline and makes no internet connections.
  • The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no in-app purchases.

Local game progress

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, boosters, language and sound preferences) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves your device, and it is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes how the app handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released.
